# Web/art sources

The SVG files are drawn for this project. The JPG photo textures below are made from public-domain (CC0 1.0) scans,
taken from the CC0 collection https://github.com/RazorEdge-wyh/unity-nature-pbr-asset-library. They were resized,
colour-graded toward the bright, saturated look of the original game and saved as JPEG. No art from the original
Dawn of Titans is used (see docs/Original_Reference.md).
